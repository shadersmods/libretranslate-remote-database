# Code of conduct

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. People with different backgrounds and levels of experience are welcome.

## Participation

Discuss the work without attacking the person. Explain disagreements with evidence or examples, accept corrections, and help new contributors find the information they need. Respect people's identities, privacy, and time.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention directed at participants, publishing private information without permission, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Maintainers may also remove spam or stop discussions that remain disruptive after a request to change course.

## Reporting

Email [neo@shadersmods.com](mailto:neo@shadersmods.com) with a description of what happened and any relevant links. You do not need to confront the person involved or make a public report. For software vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports are handled privately. Details are shared only with people who need them to assess the report or act on it, or when disclosure is legally required. Retaliation against a reporter or someone helping with a report is a violation of this policy.

## Moderation

Maintainers may remove content, ask someone to stop, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. The response should account for the behavior, its impact, and any repeated conduct. Serious threats or harassment may warrant an immediate restriction.

The same rules apply to maintainers and contributors. Anyone affected by a moderation decision may email the address above with additional context or a request for reconsideration.
